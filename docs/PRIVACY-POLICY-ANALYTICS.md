# Privacy Policy wording for Google Analytics and cookies

The Privacy Policy lives on productadvisory.co, so it has to be edited there. Paste the section below into it (a new heading near where you describe the other information you collect). It matches exactly what the site now does. Have your lawyer or legal@ read it once before you publish.

---

## Cookies and analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how our assessment, courses and website are used, for example which pages are visited and where people stop, so that we can improve them.

**What it collects.** Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and receives the pages you view, how you arrived at them, your approximate location (at city level), and your device, browser and operating system. We turn off Google's advertising features and do not use this information to show you ads. We do not send Google your name, your email address or your assessment answers.

**When it is switched on.** Google Analytics only loads after you click "Accept" on our cookie notice, or after you create an account (creating an account means you agree to this Privacy Policy). If you click "Decline", it does not load. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, we do not load it at all.

**Changing your mind.** You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of our pages or in your Profile menu. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

**How long we keep it.** Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and is then deleted automatically.

**Google's role.** Google processes this information on our behalf under its own terms. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can also install Google's opt-out add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

## Settings to match that wording (Google Analytics, about 2 minutes)

1. Open Google Analytics, click **Admin** (the gear, bottom left), choose your property.
2. **Data collection and modification → Data retention.** Set "Event data retention" to **14 months** and click **Save**. (The default is 2 months, which is also fine: if you keep 2 months, change "14 months" in the paragraph above to "2 months".)
3. **Data collection and modification → Data collection.** Make sure **Google signals data collection** is **off**. (The site already tells Google not to use it.)
4. Optional: **Data settings → Data sharing settings**, untick the boxes you do not want, such as sharing data with Google products and services.
