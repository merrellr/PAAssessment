DISC Work Styles

How do you like to work: fast or measured, focused on the task or on the people? This short check places you on those two lines and shows your style, with a report on working at your best and working well with others.

  • 24 work situations, about 8 minutes. For each, pick the response most like you, then the one least like you.
  • There are no right answers. Go with your first instinct about how you usually work, not how you’d like to.
  • You can stop at any time and pick up where you left off.

Based on William Moulton Marston’s DISC model (1928). The situations, scoring and report are Product Advisory’s own. It describes how you prefer to work, not how good you are at anything, and isn’t for hiring or selection.
