How Compass works

Why this is different

Most training is a course library and a completion report. Product Advisory Learning is built around Compass, a coach that knows where you stand and what to do next, and tells you why.

It starts with where you are

A short skill check across seven product and AI areas sets your starting level in each. You don’t sit through what you already know.

Every suggestion shows its evidence

Compass names one next step and why: your score, your recent answers and how long it takes. If you want more, ask it.

It finds the kind of gap, not just the topic

It notices which kind of question you miss: understanding the idea, applying it to a situation, making the call or reading the evidence. Knowing an idea but missing it in practice needs a different fix.

Practice adapts as you go

Two right in a row brings harder questions. Two misses brings an offer to teach the idea first. Every answer explains why.

A plan around your goals

Tell Compass what you’re working toward, by when and how much time you have. It plans each week, ticks it off as you go, and your check-in email follows it.

Honest about what it measures

Readiness comes from your skill check and your answers, area by area. It isn’t a grade or a prediction of how you’d do anywhere else, and it moves as you practise.

For teams

When your company joins

  • Admins set the company’s focus areas, and everyone’s suggestions lean toward them.
  • A team dashboard shows skills by area and progress, with a regular report by email.
  • Admins can assign courses to people, and they show up on each person’s Home.
  • People choose what their company sees; their notes and reflections stay private.
