The Training Framework

Seven Domains, 28 Competencies, Four Levels

Everything in this program is organized around seven core skill areas that make up modern product management, three of them focused specifically on working with AI. Your assessment measures where you stand in each one today, and your course plan is built from that starting point, not a generic curriculum everyone gets.

Within each domain, there are four levels: Limited Experience, Moderate Experience, Lots of Experience, and Advanced Experience. These aren't just labels, each one describes a genuinely different way of operating in that domain, not just "knows more facts."

Under each domain sit four competencies, the specific skills that make up the domain, 28 in all. Each is described at the same four levels, so you can see exactly what "Lots of Experience" in, say, Problem framing looks like.

Your skills shape

How your level in each competency is worked out

Your skill check sets the starting point for each domain's four competencies. Every practice question is tagged to the competencies its lesson builds, so each answer counts at that question's level, and lessons you finish set a floor. Each level also says how much evidence is behind it (thin, some or strong), so a level from two answers doesn't look like proof. It isn't a grade: it moves as you practise.

On My Learning › My readiness, My shape draws all 28 as a chart against a role you choose (or Compass places you), with your top three gaps and the lesson that closes each. If your company assigns you a role, your manager can see your shape against it; you're told when that happens, and your answers are never shared.
